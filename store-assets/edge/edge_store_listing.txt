Bookmark Glass is a refined bookmark management system that unites your browser bookmarks, a personal web library, and a dedicated article reading tracker into one modern macOS-inspired glassmorphic interface.

Designed for researchers, developers, and creators who want clean organization without visual clutter, Bookmark Glass keeps your native browser bookmarks and your private link library organized while offering a unified dashboard for complete management.

---

### ✨ KEY FEATURES

#### 🔖 Live Browser Bookmark & Favorites Sync
• Live Browser Synchronization: View, edit, and organize your browser's native bookmark tree. Changes made in Bookmark Glass directly update your browser bookmarks.
• Dedicated Favorites & Bookmark Bar Manager: Reorder, edit, and organize links on your browser's bookmark bar with drag-and-drop ease.
• Folder Organization with Visual Accents: Assign customizable glass color accents (Ocean Blue, Cosmic Purple, Emerald, Warm Amber, Rose Quartz, Neon Cyan) to folders without modifying native bookmark structures.
• Safe Deletions: Built-in child-item count confirmation protects you against accidental deletion of folder contents.

#### 📚 Separate Personal Library
• Your Personal Link Vault: Save important resources, tools, documentation, and references into your private library without cluttering your browser's bookmark hierarchy.
• Custom Tags & Detailed Notes: Assign multi-tag labels and rich expandable notes to any link for effortless organization.
• Favorites: Quick-star your essential links for instant one-click access.

#### 📰 Articles Reading Tracker
• Track What You Read: Dedicated reading tracker with three distinct statuses: Want to Read, In Progress, and Done Reading.
• Automatic Read Timestamps: Automatically records timestamps when an article is finished and preserves your reading history.
• Cover Image Previews: Extracts Open Graph cover images and domain metadata for a clean magazine-style view.
• Multi-Filter & Sorting Controls: Filter across statuses simultaneously and sort by Newest Saved, Oldest Saved, Recently Read, or Oldest Read.

#### ✦ Compact Glass Extension Popup
• 4-Action Navigation Bar:
  - Home: Launches the full management dashboard in a new tab.
  - Files: Browse your entire browser bookmark folder tree with instant breadcrumb navigation, drag-and-drop folder movement, and search.
  - Save: Open the smart quick-save drawer for the active webpage.
  - Change Layout: Cycle between Grid, List, and Title views on the fly.

#### ⚡ Smart Quick-Save with Duplicate Detection
• Active Tab Detection: Automatically pulls page title, URL, favicon, domain, and cover metadata from your active tab.
• Destination Picker: Choose where each item belongs: Browser Bookmarks, Personal Library, or Articles Tracker.
• Duplicate URL Warning: Alerts you if the current URL has already been saved, with options to open the existing item or save anyway.
• Inline Folder Creation: Create new bookmark or library folders on the fly directly inside the save interface.
• Instant Undo: One-click undo action directly after saving.

#### 🔍 Unified Global Search
• Keyboard-friendly search across Browser Bookmarks, Personal Library links, Saved Articles, and Folders simultaneously, complete with source badges.

#### 📐 Independent Layout Preferences
• Customize your display with Grid View, List View, or Title View independently—keep your extension popup compact in Grid view while viewing your full dashboard in List view.

#### 💾 Local Backup & Export
• Export your full library, reading list, custom folders, folder color accents, and settings to a JSON backup at any time.

---

### 🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST

• 100% Local-First Storage: All personal library links, reading history, notes, tags, and settings are stored locally on your device using browser extension storage (chrome.storage.local).
• Zero Telemetry & Zero Analytics: Bookmark Glass does not use Google Analytics, telemetry libraries, tracking beacons, or remote logging services.
• Native Local Favicons: Website icons (favicons) are retrieved entirely locally from the browser's native internal cache (via Chromium's /_favicon/ endpoint). No website URLs, domains, or IP addresses are sent to external third-party services.
• Complete Data Control: Deleting items or uninstalling the extension completely purges all local data immediately.

---

### ⌨️ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

• Ctrl+Shift+L (Command+Shift+L on macOS): Open the Bookmark Glass management dashboard directly from anywhere.

---

### 🛡️ PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

• bookmarks: Required to view, create, organize, and reorder your browser's native bookmark and favorites hierarchy.
• storage: Used to persist your personal library, article reading status, notes, tags, and layout preferences locally on your computer.
• activeTab: Used to read the current tab URL and title strictly when you invoke the extension popup or save drawer.
• scripting: Used on-demand to extract Open Graph cover images and page descriptions for saved articles.
• contextMenus: Provides quick right-click shortcut options to save links or pages to Bookmark Glass.
• favicon: Allows the extension to display website icons locally from the browser's internal cache without external network requests.
